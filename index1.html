About Local Loop

Local Loop is a community powered board where neighbors and students share skills, favors and micro-help. Whether you can tutor math, walk a dog, fix a laptop, or help someone move a couch, Local Loop connects you with people nearby who need exactly that.
The idea started on a university campus where students were already trading help informally one person editing a résumé, another lending a drill, someone else giving guitar lessons. Local Loop just gives that existing generosity a searchable, organized home.
Every post is either an Offer ("I can help with…") or a Request ("I need help with…"). No money required though members are free to arrange small thank-yous if they wish. The goal is simple: make it easy to ask for help without feeling awkward and easy to offer help without it getting lost in a group chat.
Local Loop is currently a neighborhood-and-campus pilot. It works best in tight communities where trust is already high and people actually run into each other at the grocery store.

How Local Loop Works

Post an offer or a request in under a minute
Browse by category — Tutoring, Errands, Tech Help, Creative, and more
Filter by "Offering" or "Requesting" to find exactly what you need
Mark a post as "Completed" once the favor is done
Leave a simple rating so good helpers get recognized
Report or hide any post that feels off — safety first
